About Us

Established in 2010, Al Masriya Al Emaratiya is a reliable import and export company offering comprehensive trade and commercial agency services. With our strong roots in Egypt and the UAE, we connect businesses globally through efficient logistics, transparent operations, and trusted partnerships.

Our Vision

To become a global leader in trade and logistics, renowned for excellence, trust, and sustainable growth across the cross-border corridors.

Our Mission

We aim to provide seamless international trade services that empower businesses and build long-term success. Our mission is to simplify global trade while creating value for our partners and clients.
