Trade Desk Queries

Key operational insights regarding routes, ports, and procedures

What trade commodities are supported under your corridor?

We handle heavy industrial equipment, hotel OS&E/FF&E goods, chemical raw materials (REACH-compliant), certified foodstuffs, and furniture directly through major Egyptian and UAE ports.

Do you provide Nafeza & ACI compliance assistance in Egypt?

Yes. Our in-house customs brokers manage full ACID number issuance, pre-shipment documentation, and digital cargo declaration via Egypt's single-window portal (Nafeza).

Can Al Masriya Al Emaratiya act as our official Commercial Agent?

Absolutely. We hold full commercial registration in both Egypt and the UAE, allowing us to represent global manufacturers and exporters with verified regional sales agency status.
