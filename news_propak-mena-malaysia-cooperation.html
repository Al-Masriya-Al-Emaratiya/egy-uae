On the Sidelines of ProPak: Enhancing Food Sector Trade Cooperation Between Egypt & Malaysia

June 4, 2026 Corporate Communications Global Trade, Food Division, Exhibitions
MAIN IMAGE 1
MAIN IMAGE 2

During our prominent participation in the renowned ProPak international exhibition—the premier platform for food processing, manufacturing, and packaging technologies in the region—Al Masriya Al Emaratiya for Commercial Agencies, Import & Export successfully held a series of high-level, fruitful business meetings aimed at exploring and expanding joint investment opportunities between Egypt and Malaysia within the dynamic food industry sector.

"Our presence at international forums like ProPak highlights our commitment to securing premier global partnerships that foster sustainable development and inject high-caliber supply solutions into local and regional consumer markets."

Strategic Trilateral Alignment

The strategic meetings were led by Mr. Ismail El-Menshawy, Chairman of the Board of Al Masriya Al Emaratiya, alongside senior specialists from the company's dedicated Development and Innovation Team. The delegation engaged in deep bilateral consultations with leading Malaysian corporations specializing in advanced, premium-tier raw ingredients, premix solutions, and food technology.

Key technical discussions centered around establishing robust import channels for innovative ingredient solutions, cross-border supply chain efficiencies, and certifying food products under rigorous international benchmarks including ISO and Halal qualifications. The sessions featured prominent analytical overviews with top-tier technical developers.

Diplomatic Framework & Forward Outlook

This pivotal commercial expansion was orchestrated with the gracious coordination, guidance, and support of the Malaysian Embassy officials in Egypt. Al Masriya Al Emaratiya extends its sincere appreciation to the diplomatic core for their active role in fostering bilateral trade agreements and streamlining channels that bridge leading enterprises across both nations.

By paving the way for these high-value strategic alliances, Al Masriya Al Emaratiya reinforces its structural leadership in the import, export, and commercial agency landscape, satisfying the sophisticated and growing aspirations of both the Egyptian and international markets.
